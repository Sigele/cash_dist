A simulated cash distribution platform built using Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, and AWS.

Social justice and mutual aid are causes I care deeply about. In certain online communities and social networks it is common to see those in need asking for financial assistance--usually by sharing links to a GoFundMe or Chuffed cause, or by sharing their PayPal, CashApp or Venmo information directly. 

I started this personal project--just an idea--with the aim of making that process of asking for and receiving help fast, simple, and safe.
